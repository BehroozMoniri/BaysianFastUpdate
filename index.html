Dynamic Bayesian Updating

Building adaptive machine learning models with Exponential Forgetting

About this Series

Standard Bayesian conjugate models (like Beta-Binomial or Normal-Normal) are mathematically optimal for stationary environments—meaning the underlying data never changes. However, in real-world machine learning, user behavior shifts, servers slow down, and targets move.

This series demonstrates how to introduce an exponential forgetting factor (and matrix precision discounting) to force models to "forget" outdated data. By sacrificing pure optimality, we gain the agility to track non-stationary data streams in real-time.
